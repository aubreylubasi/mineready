Privacy policy

MineReady web app, version 1.0.1. Last updated 24 November 2026.

What MineReady stores

The company details, worker details (name, job title, employee number, phone number), document details (type, reference number, issue and expiry dates, notes) and the photo or PDF copies you add.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored in your browser on the device you are using. MineReady has no server and no account. Nothing you enter is sent to MineReady or anyone else. Records on one device do not appear on another.

Data leaves your device only when you choose to share it: when you save or share a contractor file, ZIP pack or CSV export, copy a summary, or send a WhatsApp reminder. Once shared, that copy is handled by the app or person you sent it to.

Photos

Photos are re-encoded on your device before they are stored. This removes hidden information such as GPS location.

No tracking

The MineReady app loads no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, uses no cookies, and loads nothing from other websites. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your records stay until you delete them in the app or clear this website's data in your browser. Private or incognito windows do not keep records. On an iPhone, install MineReady with Share > Add to Home Screen, because Safari can clear data for websites that have not been opened for several days. Export the CSV and contractor files regularly as a backup.

Worker information

If you record details about your workers, you are responsible for having their agreement and for handling their information under the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 of Zambia.
